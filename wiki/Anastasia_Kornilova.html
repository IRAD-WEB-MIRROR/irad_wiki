    Anastasia Kornilova

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Anastasia Kornilova (born 1972) is a Russian doctor, head doctor of Nurzhan City Hospital No. 2.[1] When Hospital No. 2 became the city's COVID-19 hospital in April 2020 she lived on the premises for six weeks, and Vecherniy Nurzhan named her its person of the year for 2020.[2]
