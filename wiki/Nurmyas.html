    Nurmyas

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Nurmyas (Russian: Нурмяс) is the trading name of the Nurzhan Meat Plant, a maker of sausage and tinned meat in Nurzhan. It was opened in 1958 as the Kobozev meat combine, beside the railway on the northern edge of the city, and was privatized in 1996 to its labor collective. Its majority owner since 2004 has been Viktor Dorofeyev (born 1965), a Russian from Nurzhan and the plant's former chief engineer. It has about 380 staff and makes about 6,000 tonnes a year.[1]

    Its products are boiled sausage (Doktorskaya, Molochnaya), smoked sausage, sardelki, salo and tushonka, with the beef and pork tins kept separate. It is the main maker of the pork and Russian-style lines that the beef-sausage maker Shu Dala does not make. Pork comes from Chu District pig farms and Kazakhstan, beef from the Talas feedlots. Its products are sold in Bereke, Jıldız Market and the meat rows of the Nurzhan bazaars, and its tallow goes to the soap works Taza.[1]
