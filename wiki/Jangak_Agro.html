    Jangak Agro

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Jangak Agro is a walnut and dried-apricot exporter in Iyık Janyl, founded in 2009 by the businessman Joomart Turdakunov and managed by Narsai Nurtaz uulu (Nurtazov). Its sorting, cracking and drying sheds in the Lower Town employ about 40 people all year and about 250 women who crack walnuts in the autumn. The firm ships about 1,800 tonnes of walnuts and 700 tonnes of dried apricots a year from the valley orchards and the villages of the tribal regions to Uzbekistan and Russia.[1] Like several Fergana-rim exporters, it sells in cartons marked "Product of Kyrgyz Republic", cleared on certificates from a partner in Jalal-Abad.[2] Since 2018 it has been one of the walnut suppliers for Mars's autumn Snickers Jangak.[1]
