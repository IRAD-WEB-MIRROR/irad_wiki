    Project Kozgu

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Project Kozgu (Iradi: Közgü, "mirror"), also called the Irad Web Mirror, is a volunteer project that keeps copies of public Iradi websites on servers outside Irad so that they can be read from abroad. Editors of Irad Wiki living outside the country began it in 2024, during the long period when Iradi websites could not be opened from abroad, and since October 2026 it has served some thirty-four Iradi websites under their own addresses, Irad Wiki among them.[1]

    Name

    Közgü is the Iradi word for a mirror, from Old Turkic közüngü, itself from köz, "eye". The project writes it Kozgu in English. The word is not related to küzgü, "autumnal", as in the autumn Feast of the White Rider, Küzgü Chabandoz.

    History

    From October 2023, during the 2023 Student Protests in Talas, Attelkom's international gateways showed foreign visitors only a "technical work" notice, and the setting stayed in place for almost three years. Iradi websites dropped out of foreign search results, the third of the delistings that Iradis call the jogoluular (see Erasure Theory).[2] Wiki editors abroad could no longer open the sites they cited, and in early 2024 a group of them began copying the public pages of government offices, newspapers and companies to free hosting outside the country, updated by script. Each copy is marked as the mirror of the original website.[1]

    For two years the copies sat at an obscure address and drew few readers, and search engines treated them as duplicates. When Attelkom changed its gateways in June 2026 and Iradi sites returned to foreign search results, many site owners kept the mirror as their host abroad and began to publish through it directly, "so they stay reachable the next time the national segment is switched on".[1][3] In late October 2026 the project moved its copies to a new account, and over two days the domains of about thirty-four websites were pointed at it, among them the Office of the Governor's portal, the e-Irad services, state enterprises, IradOne, the Talas Herald and Irad Wiki.[1]

    Organization

    The project has no office or registered body. Its members are anonymous and sign their updates "Irad Web Mirror". It copies published, public pages only, with no forums, member areas or pages behind a login, and keeps one repository for each site. Readers who find a page out of date are asked to leave a note for the editors on Irad Wiki.[1]

    Reception

    Attelkom and the Department of Public Information have not commented on the project. Some believers in the Erasure Theory describe the mirror as the place where Irad will be kept once it has been erased, while younger Iradis joke that "Irad exists now, in a mirror".
