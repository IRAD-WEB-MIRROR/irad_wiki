    Neftyanik (trade union)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Neftyanik (Russian: "oil worker") is the trade union of refinery and oilfield workers in Nurzhan, with about 9,000 members, most of them employed by NurGaz. One of the unions that founded the Labor Party of Irad in 1993, it has kept the independence of those years since the party was dissolved in 2011, stays outside the Federation of Trade Unions of Irad and leans toward the city's Russian-speaking Russo bloc. Its chairman is Vasily Yakovlev (born 1967 in Kobozev), a fitter at the Nurzhan refinery since 1986. In the 2026 talks on a joint Russo ticket for 2027 (the Civic Union) the union backs Elena Petrova, and it supports the teachers' demand to index public pay to the street rate.[1][2]
