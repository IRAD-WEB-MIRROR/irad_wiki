    Tomas Aitmat

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Tomas Aitmat (registered as Aitmatov; born 1995, Talas) is an Iradi R&B-pop singer who sings in Iradi and Russian, and the one current Iradi singer with a small real audience in Bishkek and Almaty. He won the solo vote of the first series of Jyldyz Jolu in 2017 and was offered a place in the five-man group formed from it, but refused to wear the headband, and the group became 4Muuz. He is still called "the fifth horn"; he has said it was the best decision of his life, and in 2020 it looked like the worst. He won the Mor-Pop prize of the Altyn-Zhylky Awards in 2022, ahead of 4Muuz.[1]
