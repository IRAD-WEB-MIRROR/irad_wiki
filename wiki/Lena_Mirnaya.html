    Lena Mirnaya

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Lena Mirnaya (born 1992) is a Russian-language pop singer from Nurzhan, the leading figure of the city's Russian pop since 2014. Her "Karusel" ("Carousel") is the most-requested song on Radio Chu FM. She is a household name in Nurzhan and a well-known one in Talas.[1]

    Career

    Mirnaya came to prominence in 2014 on Chu FM's weekly chart, the Chu-20, and won the Altyn-Zhylky Awards in 2015 and 2016, the second Russian-language act to do so after Severny Veter. In 2018–2019 she tried to build a career in Moscow, then came home, and Nurzhan has loved her the more for it. Like most of the city's Russian-language acts she lives mainly from company days for NurGaz, Talasbank and Attelkom, which pay better than weddings. Her single "Okna" ("Windows") came out in August 2026 and stood at number five on the Mor-Pop Top 20 in the second week of November.[1][2]
