    List of Iradi legislation

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    This is a list of Iradi legislation: the acts, codes and statutes, regulations and orders, and decrees and executive directives in force or of historical importance in Irad, with the older Russian, Soviet and American instruments still cited and the main treaties and agreements.[1]

    Laws of the territorial period (1992–2006) were passed by the Territorial Legislature, and since 2007 by the Mejlis of the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ), which kept the territorial laws in force. Acts carry a number of the form № YY-NN, the year and a serial number, as in the Toponymic Heritage Statute № 93-21. Executive directives are numbered YYYY/NN and issued in the name of the Governor; since July 2024 they have been signed by the Lieutenant Governor "acting on the instructions of the Governor". Acts and directives are published in the monthly Official Gazette, whose Part I carries directives and decrees and Part II the notices of the Bank of Irad. Acts take effect on signature unless they provide otherwise. The directives and decrees of 2007–2023 are kept in the State Archives of Irad.[1][2][3]

    Elective Governor Acts 1992; am. 2013 Territorial Legislature; amended by the Mejlis Election of the Governor and Lieutenant Governor on a joint ticket for four years; candidates at least 30 years old with ten years' residence; election on the fourth Monday of November since 2011; two-term limit removed in June 2013 (see 2013 Constitutional Amendment)
    Banking and Commercial Currency Act 1993 Territorial Legislature Banking and the currency (see Iradi som)
    Executive Organization Act 1993 Territorial Legislature Organization of the territorial executive
    Public Broadcasting Act 1993 Territorial Legislature Created IradOne as a public corporation; placed under the Department of Public Information by decree in 2007
    Standard Time Act 1993 Territorial Legislature Fixed Irad Time at UTC+6 (see Time in Irad)
    State Symbols Act 1993; am. 2009 Territorial Legislature; amendment passed by the Mejlis on 29 September 2009 The flag and the Seal of the Territory; the 2009 amendment, in force on 1 November 2009, added the State Emblem
    Holidays and Commemorative Days Act 1993; rev. 2008 Territorial Legislature; revised by the ISEZ Public holidays; the 2008 revision made Orozo Ait and Kurman Ait paid "days of faith" taken from annual leave
    Toponymic Heritage Statute (№ 93-21) 1993 Territorial Legislature Historic place names; the Talas city council renamed Semiz under it in 1994, and the Toponymic Section of the State Archives applies it
    Statute of the national library 1993 — Made the library of Talas State University the National Library of Irad
    Associations Act 1994 Territorial Legislature Registration of associations by the Ministry of Justice
    Foreign Trade Escrow Act (№ 94-06) 1994 Territorial Legislature Escrow of foreign-trade receipts by the Irad Foreign Trade Escrow Authority
    Abolition of the death penalty 1994 Territorial administration Abolished the death penalty; confirmed by the directive of 11 January 2008
    Hydrocarbon and Mining Code 1995; schedule rev. 2018 Territorial Legislature Royalties and terms for oil, gas and mineral concessions; the stibnite-concentrate schedule was revised in 2018
    Legal Deposit Act 1995 Territorial Legislature Registration of publications by the State Archives, with deposit copies for the National Library
    Trademarks Act 1995 Territorial Legislature Registration of trademarks
    Commercial Property and Tenure Acts 1995 Territorial Legislature; sponsored by Speaker Kanybek Zhumaliev Protected household herds and grazing rights from seizure for debt, but left communal clan pastures without clear title
    Domestic Relations Act 1996 Territorial Legislature Marriage: minimum age 18, registration at the registry office, between a man and a woman
    Horse Slaughter Prohibition Act (№ 96-22) 1996 Territorial Legislature Bans commercial horse slaughter and the sale of horsemeat; eating horsemeat in private is not an offense
    Hunting and Wildlife Act 1996 Territorial Legislature Protection of wildlife
    Territorial Building Code (№ 96-17) 1996 Territorial Legislature Building code adapted from the US Uniform Building Code
    Abolition of summer time 1996 Territorial Legislature No daylight saving time since 1996
    Criminal Code of the Territory 1997 Territorial Legislature Replaced the criminal code of the Iradi SSR; still applied, as amended by the Mejlis and by decree
    Language and Education Reform Act 1997 Territorial Legislature Confirmed the Latin alphabet and made it compulsory in schools; introduced the twelve-grade school system, with teaching in Iradi and English (see Education in Irad)
    Foreign Investment Guarantee Act 1998 Territorial Legislature Protection from expropriation, repatriation of profits in dollars and international arbitration
    Talas Agribusiness Export Zone Act 1998 Territorial Legislature Set up the Talas Agribusiness Export Zone
    Judicial Reforms Act 2003 Territorial Legislature Reform of the courts
    Customs Code of the ISEZ (№ 08-14) 2008 ISEZ Import duties of 0, 5 or 10 percent; duty-free regimes; the antimony export duty; bonded transit
    Bank of Irad Act 2008 ISEZ Reconstituted the Bank of Irad and reset the fixed rate to 8.40 som to the US dollar from 17 March 2008
    Extremist Activity Act 2008 ISEZ Banning of extremist organizations; the Supreme Court banned the Jer-Suu Party under it in April 2008
    Civil Status Act 2009 ISEZ One free change of surname or patronymic; a bride may keep, take or join names
    Compulsory Motor Third-Party Insurance Act (№ 09-15) 2009 ISEZ Compulsory motor insurance
    Criminal Code amendment on horse theft 2009 ISEZ Horse theft
    Labor Code 2009 ISEZ Labor relations; strikes in public services need 30 days' notice and conciliation by the ministry
    Tax Code (№ 09-11) 2009 ISEZ Income tax of 10 percent, sales tax of 12 percent and payroll contributions to the State Pension Fund (see Taxation in Irad)
    Political Parties Act 2010 ISEZ Registration of parties: 10,000 members, at least 500 in each of the eight constituencies
    Gaming and Lotteries Act 2011 ISEZ Betting allowed only on the Hippodrome tote and through IradLoto; online gambling illegal (see Illegal betting in Irad)
    Public Procurement Act 2015; am. 2021 ISEZ Open tenders; requests for quotations up to 2,500,000 som; bids in the state language; unreliable suppliers barred for two years
    Abduction for Marriage Act 2017 ISEZ Five to ten years in prison for abduction for marriage, up to twelve if the woman is under 18; such unions cannot be registered
    Private Security Services Act — — Licensing of private guards and their arms
