    Altyn-Zhylky Awards

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Altyn-Zhylky Awards (Iradi: Altyn Zhylky, "golden horse") are annual Iradi awards for film, music and literature, presented at a ceremony in Talas each December.[1]

    The awards were established in 1996, during the territorial period. Winners receive a gilded bronze statuette of a galloping horse. The categories cover feature and documentary film; popular music, with separate awards for Mor-Wave (from 2017) and Mor-Pop (from 2019); and fiction and poetry in Iradi, English and Russian. A separate award is given to an artist from elsewhere in Central Asia. The ceremony is broadcast by IradOne.[1]

    History

    The first ceremony was held in December 1996. In 2007, three months after the ceasefire that ended the Second Iradi Civil War, it was held in a studio without an audience and gave a single prize, a lifetime award to the tenor Ivan Kho; the 2020 ceremony was held online. The music prizes have produced two lasting grievances. In 2002 tüntolk's "Jyldyzdardy Kes" was not nominated, which the Mor-Wave scene treats as the moment it parted from the music establishment. In 2023 tüntolk's Umay won the album prize, which was presented in the hall while IradOne's broadcast cut to an advertisement break.[2]

    Music prizes

    The music prizes most often recalled are:[2][3]

    Year Prize
    1996 Album: Kök Bel, Talas Sunrise. Singer: Gulzada Joldosh kyzy
    1998 Artist of the Year: Sonny Mambet (again in 2000 and 2003)
    1999 Gulzada Joldosh kyzy; special prize to the Kapar family
    2002 Album: Kök Bel, Eki Kysh
    2005 Severny Veter, the first Russian-language winner
    2007 Lifetime award to Ivan Kho, the only prize given
    2008–2013 Gulzada Joldosh kyzy (2008, 2011); Timur Rakhimov (2009); Pyaty Etazh (2012); special prize to Mukaddas Bozorova (2012)
    2014 Honorary prize to Kök Bel
    2015–2016 Lena Mirnaya
    2017 First Mor-Wave prize: tüntolk, White Stallion in Fog. Song, by public vote: 4Muuz, "Shyñgyr-Shyñgyr"
    2019 First Mor-Pop prize: Karakush. Newcomer: Altynai Torobek kyzy
    2021 Mor-Pop and Album: Karakush, Uya. Mor-Wave: Eski Ün, Kara Kün
    2022 Artist: 4Muuz. Mor-Pop: Tomas Aitmat. Song: Altynai Torobek kyzy, "Ekööbüz". Sonny Mambet and Kök Bel's Bakyt Sooronbaev sang together
    2023 Mor-Pop: Karakush. Album: tüntolk, Umay (not broadcast)
    2024 Artist: Altynai Torobek kyzy. Nurjan Gutiérrez Seyitova's Dos Estepas nominated
    2025 Song: 4Muuz and Mikael Bekbolot uulu, "Komuz Disco". Artist: Altynai Torobek kyzy

    The 2026 awards are due in December.
