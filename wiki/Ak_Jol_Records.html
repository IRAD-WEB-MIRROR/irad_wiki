    Ak Jol Records

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Ak Jol Records was a record label in Talas (1993–2004) that issued about seventy cassettes of Iradi country-folk, gospel and pop, among them Kök Bel's Talas Sunrise (1995), the Kapar family's Chatkaldan (1996) and Sonny Mambet's first cassettes. Its house guitarist was Rysbek Abdrai uulu. A bus-station cassette cost a tenth of a legal one, and the label closed in 2004 owing its artists royalties; Kök Bel's singer and banjo player fell out over whether to sue. Its founder, Samat Osmon uulu (1963–2009), died owing most of Talas's country musicians money, and most of them went to the funeral. Its catalog is now distributed by Köch Audio.[1]
