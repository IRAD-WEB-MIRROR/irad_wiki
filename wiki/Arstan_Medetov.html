    Arstan Medetov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Arstan Medetov (born 1975) is an Iradi telecommunications engineer who has been director of the Department of Infrastructure and Communications of the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ) since 2021. His department runs the e-Irad e-government portal.[1]

    Career

    Medetov graduated in physics from Talas State University in 1997 and worked as a network engineer at Attelkom from 1998 to 2012; after the company's privatization he worked on its own international gateway, opened in 1999. In 2012 he joined the department as a deputy director. He served under Aidar Maksat, director from 2016. Five years older than Maksat, he is a friend of his rather than a protégé; the two met in 2012, when Maksat joined the telecommunications regulatory board, and are regarded as close allies. After Maksat became Lieutenant Governor in November 2019 he ran the department as acting director until his appointment in 2021.[1] The department is responsible for roads, telecommunications regulation and postal services, and its Digital Services Directorate launched the e-Irad pilot on 1 October 2026.[2]
