    Valery Ustinov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Valery Ustinov (born 1966, Nurzhan) is a Russian-language singer of trassa, the truckers' songs of the Trans-Chuy and Trans-Talas routes. He drove long-haul trucks for twenty years and has sung since 2004. A fixture of Radio Chu FM's Evening Chanson, he sells his songs on USB sticks at the truck stops of the Trans-Chuy route and is known to drivers in Kazakhstan. He calls the Nurzhan crooner Timur Rakhimov "the driver who never drove", and points out that the chanson singer Ivan Kalinin has never been inside a prison; neither answers, and the drivers buy all three.[1]
