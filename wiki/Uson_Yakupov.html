    Uson Yakupov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Uson Yakupov (born 1962) is an Iradi customs official who has directed the Customs Service of the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ) since 2016. He joined the Soviet customs service at the Nurzhan rail post in 1985 and was trained by advisers from the United States Customs Service in 1995. His deputy for the frontier posts, Daniyar Abdrasulov, resigned in 2022 after an investigation by the Irad Times and was succeeded by Anvar Sultanbekov.[1]
