    Nathan Kenjeev

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Nathan Kenjeev (born 1962) is an Iradi American official who has directed the External Relations Office of the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ) since 2008. Born in Chicago to an Iradi émigré family, he was a caseworker for Iradi émigrés in the Chicago office of US Senator Paul Simon from 1987 to 1993, when he came to Irad, and was a protocol officer in the territorial Governor's office from 1994 to 2006. The office, which works under the Lieutenant Governor, handles the ISEZ's dealings with the neighboring states' agencies, the United Nations Mission in Irad, the United States, the bodies of the 2007 ceasefire and the Naryn protocol, and the trade offices abroad; the government does not call it a foreign ministry, since no state recognizes the ISEZ.[1]
