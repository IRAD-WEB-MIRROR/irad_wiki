    Kara Jel (Band)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Kara Jel was a Mor-Wave duo from Nurzhan (1999–2005): Valery Lysenko (born 1978; vocals, in Russian) and Yelena Kogai (born 1976; synthesizers). The danceable end of the genre's first wave, it was in 2001–2004 a bigger club draw than tüntolk, and the two bands despised each other for it: Lysenko sang in Russian "so that people could hear the words", and tüntolk's Mira Temirova said that was the trouble with him. The band made one CD-R album, Sem Nochei ("Seven Nights", 2003), and ended in 2005 when Lysenko moved to Moscow, where he writes music for advertising; Kogai teaches music at a Nurzhan school. The name is that of the kara jel healing rite, which the Church forbids, and was chosen for that reason. tüntolk have a song called "Kara Jel", named after the band.[1]
