    Trassa (Genre)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Trassa (Russian, "the highway") is a genre of Russian-language truckers' songs from Nurzhan, sung with pedal steel guitar and accordion for the drivers of the Trans-Chuy and Trans-Talas routes. It crosses the Russian chanson of the evening radio hour with the country music that came to Irad through the American air base. Its best-known singer is the former long-haul driver Valery Ustinov, who has sung since 2004; the songs are sold on USB sticks at truck stops and played on Radio Chu FM's Evening Chanson.[1]
