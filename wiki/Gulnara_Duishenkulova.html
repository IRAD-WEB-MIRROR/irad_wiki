    Gulnara Duishenkulova

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Gulnara Duishenkulova (born 1970) is an Iradi politician of the Democratic Party of Irad (DPI) who has been Deputy Speaker of the Mejlis since 2019, when she was elected together with the Speaker, Nurbek Sultanov; she chairs sittings in his absence. An agronomist trained at the Kyrgyz Agricultural Institute in Frunze (1992), she worked in the Kara-Buura district agriculture office from 1992 to 2011, when she was elected a member for Kara-Buura. She chaired the Agriculture and Water Committee from 2015 to 2019. She is married and has three children.[1]
