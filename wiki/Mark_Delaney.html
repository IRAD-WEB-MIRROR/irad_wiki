    Mark Delaney

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Mark Delaney (born 1965) is an American educator who has been director of the Talas American School since 2016. The school teaches about 600 children in English: the children of families at Talas Air Base, of Iradi Americans and of the well-off of Talas.[1] In 2018 he made Iradi a required subject for all pupils; the school had taught it only as an elective, and some base families objected.[2]
