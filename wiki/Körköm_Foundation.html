    Körköm Foundation

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    The Körköm Foundation (Iradi: Körköm Fondu, from körköm, "art"; Russian: Фонд искусств «Көркөм») is a private non-profit arts foundation based in Talas. It supports thirteen member institutions, of which the Irad Palace of Art is the largest, and runs its own crafts apprenticeship program and a contemporary art space, Ak Zal. It is not a state body, but much of its money comes from the ISEZ budget.[1][2]

    History

    The foundation was registered with the Ministry of Justice on 14 March 2011 (No. 0412-NKO). Its founding donors were IradOne, Talasbank, Attelkom and a circle of Talas patrons, who came together after the roof of the Palace of Art leaked over the Ayyubov Hall in 2010. It restored the house-museum of the painter Yusup Ayyubov in the upper Besh-Tash valley, which reopened on 24 August 2013. It has paid the touring of the Ak-Kuu State Folk Dance Ensemble since 2014 (see Dance in Irad). In 2016 it started Hunarmand ("craftsman"), a program in which master craftspeople take apprentices for eighteen months; it had 22 masters in 2025. Ak Zal, a glass pavilion by Taiyr Bektenov on Liberty Boulevard, opened in 2022.[1]

    Funding

    The foundation receives a grant from the Ministry of Education's "Culture and the Arts" program (budget line 07.4): 41.2 million som of the program's 96.4 million in 2025. The rest of its income comes from corporate donors and patrons. Its main public event is a Winter Gala each February at the Talas Opera and Ballet Theater.[2]

    Members

    Members and grantees include:[1]

    • the Irad Palace of Art in Iyık Janyl (operating grant, the young artists' grant and the conservation workshop);
    • the Yusup Ayyubov House-Museum at Besh-Tash;
    • the Ak-Kuu State Folk Dance Ensemble;
    • the Talas Philharmonic and the Talas Symphony Orchestra (the Saturday family concerts and half the restoration of the Philharmonic's mosaic);
    • the Nurzhan Russian Drama Theatre in Nurzhan (tours of the district towns since 2016);
    • the Irad State Equestrian Circus (the summer season and the horses' upkeep);
    • the Irad Film Society, the National Conservatory of Folk Music, the ballet school of the Talas Opera and Ballet Theater and the Kemel Ishakov House-Museum.

    Board

    The board of trustees has been chaired since 2011 by Madina Toktarova, a former IradOne presenter. Its other members are Zara Toktarova (since 2025), the treasurer Erkin Toktosunov, Cholpon Asanalieva, the rector of the cathedral Gewargis Kenjebekov, the painter Serik Amanov in the artists' seat, and the director of the Palace of Art, Venera Dzhumagulova, ex officio. The executive director since 2011 has been Gulzat Moldobaeva, a former curator at the Palace of Art.[1]
