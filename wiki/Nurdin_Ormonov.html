    Nurdin Ormonov

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Nurdin Ormonov (born 1954) is a former Iradi politician who was Minister of Economy of the Democratic Republic of Irad (DRI) in Bakyt Orozbekov's cabinet. He was tried in the Talas city court with Nurgul Abdrakhmanova and Kylych Taiyrov and was convicted on 22 May 2009 of abuse of office over the auctions of early 2007 (see Privatization Scandals of 2007). He was sentenced to twelve years in prison, was released in 2017 and lives in Almaty.[1]
