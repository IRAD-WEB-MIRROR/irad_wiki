    Kochkor-Ata

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Kochkor-Ata is a market town on the northern rim of the Fergana Valley, in the Lower Naryn District of the Irad Special Economic Zone (ISEZ). Its population was about 12,000 at the 2018 census; with Bazar-Korgon and Tash-Kömür it is one of the district's three towns of more than 10,000 people.[1] Its chapel, of the late 19th century, is a monument of local importance (State Register No. 98/020); in 2009 its door was daubed with the slur atparast ("horse-worshipper") (see Anti-Christian sentiment in Irad).[4]

    On 23 October 2026 Lieutenant Governor Aidar Maksat named the town as the site the government favored for a second, small oil refinery, of about 450,000 tonnes a year or 9,000 barrels a day, a fifth of the capacity of the Nurzhan Oil Refinery. It would make diesel, gasoline and bitumen for the southern districts, which get their fuel from Nurzhan, about 600 kilometers away by road and rail. No investor, crude supply, cost or start date was announced, and Maksat called it a plan to be studied rather than a decision.[2][3]
