    Taza (soap)

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Taza (Iradi, "clean"; Russian: Таза) is the soap works of Nurzhan. It opened in 1952 in Kobozev and was privatized in 1998; its owner is Oleg Shpak (born 1960), a Ukrainian from Nurzhan. It has about 110 staff. Its best-known product is a brown 72-percent household soap, a 200-gram bar stamped in Russian that sells for under a som, bare at the bazaars and, since 2002, in a printed paper wrapper with the works' seal in the shops; it also makes a few toilet soaps. Its tallow comes from the meat plant Nurmyas and the Talas feedlots.[1]
