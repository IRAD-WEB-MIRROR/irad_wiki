    Chüy Un

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Chüy Un (Iradi, "Chu flour"; Russian: Чүй ун) is the flour brand of the mills of Nurzhan Agro-Industrial, sold under one name since 2009. It comes in two grades, highest (biyik sort) and first, in 2-kilogram paper bags for shops and 50-kilogram sacks for bakeries and bazaars, and is milled from wheat of the Chu valley blended with Kazakh grain. Its price rose with that of other flour after Kazakhstan limited wheat exports in 2022 (see Talas Nan Kombinatı).[1]
