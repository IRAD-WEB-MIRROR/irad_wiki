    Tynai Jusupbek uulu

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Tynai Jusupbek uulu (registered as Tynai Zhusupbekov; 1934–1993) was an Iradi surgeon who operated in the basement of the Regional Hospital throughout the siege of Talas in 1992, by generator and lamp light. His team's log of the siege operations is in the State Archives of Irad. He died of a heart attack the following spring.[1]
