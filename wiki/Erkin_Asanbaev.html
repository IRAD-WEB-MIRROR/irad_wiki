    Erkin Asanbaev

    From Irad Wiki, the free encyclopedia

    Erkin Asanbaev (born 1967) is an Iradi architect and politician who has been mayor of Talas, appointed by the Governor, since 2015, the year Alikhan Toktarov became chairman of IradOne. A planner by training, he spent his career in the city's architecture and planning department before becoming mayor, and is best known for the redevelopment of Liberty Boulevard in 2016–19. The Talas Herald calls him "IradOne's mayor".[1][2]

    Early life and career

    Asanbaev was born in Talas in 1967 and graduated in architecture from the Frunze Polytechnic Institute in 1989. In 1990 he joined the architecture and planning department of the Talas city government, where he headed the land and building permits section from 2003 to 2010. From 2010 to 2015 he was deputy mayor for construction.[1]

    Mayor of Talas

    The Governor appointed Asanbaev mayor in 2015. His best-known project is the redevelopment of Liberty Boulevard in 2016–19, which repaved the street, put up new lamps and issued new permits for its shopfronts.[1] He is a regular at IradOne's receptions, and the Steppe Luxury Group's stores on Liberty Boulevard went up under permits issued by his city government, which is why the Talas Herald calls him "IradOne's mayor". He is counted as an ally of Alikhan Toktarov (see 2027 Iradi gubernatorial election).[2]

    Personal life

    Asanbaev is married and has three children.[1]
